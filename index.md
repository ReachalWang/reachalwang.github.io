## Research Interests

- **Prompt Injection Attacks & Defenses**: automatically red-teaming agentic systems with prompt injection attacks, and quickly detecting and responding to malicious injections.
- **Safety of Long-Horizon Agents**: understanding and reducing the risks that build up as agents act over many steps, including how they manage memory and context, use tools, and interact with other agents.
